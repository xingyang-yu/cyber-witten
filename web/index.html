🔭 Cyber-Witten

Semantic search over Edward Witten's arXiv papers. Ask a physics question and this finds the most relevant passages, ranked by meaning. It is a finder, not a chatbot: it returns source passages with arXiv links, it does not write an answer. The answer-writing version is the local app.
Everything runs in your browser — no server, no API, nothing sent anywhere.
